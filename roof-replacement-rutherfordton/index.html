What roof replacement can involve

A replacement commonly involves removing existing roofing materials, checking the surface beneath them, and putting new roofing components in place. The exact scope depends on the roof and the condition found during the work. Ask whether damaged areas beneath the old material could change the plan, and how any additional work would be discussed with you.

Before choosing materials, consider the roof’s shape, the look you want for the house, and any practical preferences you have. Ask how the selected materials are intended to work together as a complete roofing system. Get the scope in writing so you can refer back to the agreed work.

Mistakes to avoid include assuming every roof needs full replacement, accepting a vague description of the job, or overlooking cleanup and disposal when discussing the scope. If you see a new leak or a change in the roof’s appearance, contact a professional rather than attempting a roof-level inspection.
